Sculptor & Visual Artist

Form, Material & Expression

Rooted in the rich artistic soil of Egypt, I am a sculptor driven by a deep fascination with the human form and the raw, tactile language of materials. My practice is a constant exploration of the space between classical figurative sculpture and conceptual expression. From terracotta anatomical studies to bold, experimental pieces, my work translates emotion, identity, and raw form into physical mass.
